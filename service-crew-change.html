Crew changes & welfare

Support the people behind the voyage.

All services ←Discuss this service ↗

What the service involves

Crew-change planning connects joining and departing personnel with travel, transfer, receiving and vessel schedules. A clear brief identifies the movement, location, timing and responsible contacts while sensitive personal documents move through an agreed channel.

How coordination works

Share the vessel, nominated port, crew movement outline and desired attendance window. Identify the principal who can approve arrangements. The vessel schedule, access requirements and travel details must be coordinated rather than treated as separate bookings.

From enquiry to handover

Welfare requests may involve provisions, shore arrangements or coordination with qualified medical providers. Explain the requirement and urgency so the appropriate parties can assess it. Medical decisions remain with qualified professionals.

Why a connected approach matters

Information has value when the next team can act on it. AnchorVoyage brings the request back to a clear objective, an agreed scope and understandable responsibilities. Identify the decision owner, the conditions for attendance and the record that will close the assignment.

Start the conversation

  • Vessel, cargo or project reference
  • Intended Nigerian port and facility
  • Required timing and objective
  • Supporting specifications where relevant
  • Responsible principal and approval contact
