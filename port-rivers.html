Start with the actual facility.

The port name gives the enquiry a location. The nominated terminal, vessel purpose and cargo particulars give it an operational direction. Share those details with your arrival estimate, required services and principal contact.

Connect the requirements.

Agency, crew support, provisions, technical attendance, cargo documentation and inland delivery can interact within the same call. Agree the scope, receiving arrangements and approval responsibilities before work is committed.

Keep the next step clear.

Useful updates distinguish an estimate from a confirmed event. Identify what remains outstanding, who can decide and which activity depends on the answer. Vessel, terminal, authority and specialist responsibilities remain with the appointed parties.

  • Port and nominated terminal or facility
  • Estimated arrival or cargo readiness
  • Requested services and priorities
