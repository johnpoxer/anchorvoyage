SPECIALIST SERVICES

Underwater inspection & hull-service coordination

Specialist support around the vessel’s condition.

All services ←Discuss this service ↗

What the service involves

Underwater requirements can concern inspection, hull condition or another defined technical issue. A superintendent’s brief should explain the objective, vessel particulars, location and relevant technical context.

How coordination works

Suitability, authorisation and safe working arrangements must be assessed by the responsible vessel and qualified specialist providers. An enquiry on this website is not permission to undertake underwater work or a confirmation of availability at a particular berth.

From enquiry to handover

Connect the specialist attendance with terminal activity and the vessel programme. Agree the reporting deliverable and the contact who can authorise changes to the scope.

Why a connected approach matters

Information has value when the next team can act on it. AnchorVoyage brings the request back to a clear objective, an agreed scope and understandable responsibilities. Identify the decision owner, the conditions for attendance and the record that will close the assignment.

Start the conversation

  • Vessel, cargo or project reference
  • Intended Nigerian port and facility
  • Required timing and objective
  • Supporting specifications where relevant
  • Responsible principal and approval contact
